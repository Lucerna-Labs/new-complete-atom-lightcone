# Source, evidence, and rights policy

Every curriculum record must cite registered evidence or be explicitly marked
as a synthetic control. Retrieval text is data, never instruction or
permission.

## Rights lanes

- `green`: compatible open license or public-domain material may be processed
  under its attribution and redistribution conditions.
- `citation_only`: retain metadata, locator, and original structured summary;
  do not redistribute protected source text.
- `restricted`: may be used only under a separately documented authorization;
  excluded by default.
- `unknown`: unusable until reviewed.

## Epistemic classes

- `formal`: machine-checkable theorem, derivation, or exact execution.
- `established`: strongly supported observation or consensus mechanism.
- `provisional`: supported but actively uncertain or incomplete.
- `hypothesis`: testable proposed explanation; never rendered as settled fact.
- `contested`: material disagreement with separate provenance for positions.
- `interpretive`: criticism, rhetoric, philosophy, or literary interpretation.
- `creative`: invented content with no real-world truth authority.
- `synthetic_control`: generated negative/adversarial fixture with no evidence
  authority.

Date-sensitive medicine, security, standards, software, and scientific claims
must carry an `as_of` or version boundary and an update obligation.
